First public release: Valve's NextBot/TFBot AI for Fortress Forever 2013 dedicated servers (Windows), as a Metamod:Source plugin. Replaces the legacy FF bot.

### Highlights
- Understands FF's Lua game modes through a small census script (`maps/globalscripts/ffnb_botinfo.lua`): CTF, attack/defend, invade/defend, command points, ball, Hunted and deathmatch, with up to four teams and alliances.
- FF classes and weapons, buildings through FF's commands, medkit healing, engineer armour repair, spy disguise, hand grenades.
- Nav meshes for 19 maps (separate download), and `ffnb_nav_generate` for others.
