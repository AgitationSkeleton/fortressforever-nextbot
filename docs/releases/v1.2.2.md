- **Four-team nav blocking:** blocked state is tracked per team number. Before, yellow shared team 2's flags (and green shared blue's), so a door blocked for one also sealed the other inside its own spawn. Team-owned doors now block every team that is neither the owner nor allied to it.
- **ff_hunted assassins leave their spawns:** doors that are themselves Lua team doors (touch-opened yellow spawn doors) belong to that team instead of counting as walls.
