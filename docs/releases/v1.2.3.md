### Fixes
Found by looking for spots where bots spend a large share of their time without moving (server heatmaps).

- **Spawn doors opened by map Lua:** a door controlled by a Lua `trigger_ff_script` (for example `red_spawn1door_trigger`) counted as a wall for every team, because only `trigger_multiple` was recognised as a door trigger. Bots on maps like ff_roasted_classic stood at their spawn exits all game. A `trigger_ff_script` with "door" in its name (location triggers excluded) now controls the door and gives it the trigger's team.
- **Home flag with no path:** a bot sent to its own flag at home stood at the end of a partial path forever (ff_well: every bot at a ladder foot). If the path ends more than 200 units short of a home flag, the bot gives up and hunts enemies for a while instead.
- **Nav seeding:** `ffnb_nav_seed_ladders` also seeds unmeshed flags, capture points, command points, hunted escapes and balls, so their rooms get nav areas when a mesh is regenerated.

### Known gaps
- Bots don't ride lifts that map Lua drives (ff_well's flag rooms, ff_fusion's defender spawn).
- On ff_ksour, ff_cornfield and ff_napoli a flag carrier can stall near the capture point.
